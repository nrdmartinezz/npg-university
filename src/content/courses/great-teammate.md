Culture is built one teammate at a time. Brian Wright asks you to look at the attitudes, behaviors, and actions that either strengthen the team or quietly undermine it. Living “above the line” means taking personal responsibility, reinforcing unity, and contributing to an environment where people and plans can work.

A strong culture decides whether plans succeed or stall. The course contrasts traits that energize high-performing teams with behaviors that erode morale, including blame, gossip, and complacency. It covers how to shift from reactive to proactive behavior, strengthen trust, and contribute to unity across the practice. The examples are meant to turn those ideas into daily actions.

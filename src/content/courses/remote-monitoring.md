Join Founder and CEO Brian Wright for three essential remote-monitoring talking points aimed at conversion, starts, and revenue, and at what the practice is able to charge.

The course is for practices already using remote patient monitoring and for practices considering it. It teaches a specific way to showcase and talk about remote monitoring so it can increase starts, and how to verbalize each talking point. You keep access for life.

## Skill sets

- Leadership and culture
- Hospitality
- Listening and speaking with intent
- Sales fundamentals and strategies
- Consumer psychology
- Verbiage and presentation

## Modules

- **Introduction.** Why to reimagine exam processes and use remote monitoring to get more starts at a higher treatment fee.
- **The art of discovery.** Asking and listening with intent.
- **Verbiage to avoid.** The “domino effect,” the order of what you say, and how everyday language can lead to a no.
- **Transitional phrase — control and empathy.** Staying in control of the conversation, and the psychology of consensus.
- **Better, more efficient clinical results.** How to articulate a better in-treatment experience.
- **More personal attention.** A different way to talk about remote monitoring than the default.
- **Convenience of your schedule.** The third talking point.
- **Putting it all together.** A script that recaps the course.

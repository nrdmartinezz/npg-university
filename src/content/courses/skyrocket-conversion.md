Join Founder and CEO Brian Wright for this 53-minute on-demand course on transforming the financial presentation. The sales page calls it a system for the results you want without the usual headaches.

## Course agenda

- **Introduction.** How consumer buying tendencies affect the decision to accept a treatment plan.
- **The insurance dilemma.** People shop more than ever, and coverage may look the same at the next practice. The lesson is about presenting so your practice adds more value than the others they are comparing.
- **Anchoring bias.** How this idea applies across the practice and to conversion.
- **Sticker shock.** Most consumers buy through monthly payments, and the lesson covers a specific way to say the price.
- **The down payment dilemma.** How the down payment can crush conversion, and how presenting it differently can bring more of it in.
- **Trigger words.** Words to avoid when presenting money.
- **Personal bias.** How the presenter’s own bias gets in the way, and how to get past it.
- **The 0% interest dilemma.** Why 0% interest is not automatically the best option for the practice, and how to present the option that works for the business.
- **Rethinking treatment-time payment plans.** How not extending payment plans can lower conversion when people decide on a monthly number.
- **Affordability versus being cheap.** How to increase prices while staying affordable.
- **Unintentionally driving prices down.** Presentation mistakes that keep you from raising prices and conversion.

Orthodontic and dental practices compete for patients who have more options than ever. This course is about standing out by making a real connection and creating a wow experience through the team’s intentional work.

New Patient Group’s New Patient Experience course is on-demand training for the visit itself. With nearly 6 hours of video, Founder and CEO Brian Wright teaches sales, presentation, customer service, digital touchpoints, and objection handling.

The sales page describes it as innovative sales, presentation, psychology, customer service, and hospitality training designed to increase starts, cash flow, efficiency, and organic referrals, while reducing no-shows, advertising costs, and headaches.

## Curriculum

### Introduction

- Redefining experience for the new economy
- Learning to go beyond

### OSM — Operational Success Meeting

- Essential rules for success
- Building an agenda

### The Communication and Customer Service Meeting (CCSM)

- Mistakes to avoid and rules for success
- A positive start
- New patient experience and existing patient experience
- Yesterday in review, social media, demonstration, and CCSM scripting

### Ambience and digitalization

- Patient forms and contracts, practice visibility
- Parking lot to front door, front desk, and waiting area
- iTero scanning, smart board monitor, exam room, and remote monitoring

### Down time

- Doctor welcome video and tips for success
- Doctor wow factor
- Verification and early communication of insurance benefits
- TC welcome video

### VIP greeting

- Team exercise, demonstration, and the psychology of the greeting
- A step-by-step process, scripting, and knowing your patient
- The red carpet roll out, a no-wait policy, and a VIP welcome gift

### Value tour

- Don’t waste time, and be quick without rushing
- Team introductions, wall of fame, personalization, and the community wall
- Cleanliness, organization, and passion
- Touch points: waiting room, bathrooms, brushing station, x-ray, sterilization, lab, and clinic

### Final exam

- Testing that covers the on-demand video

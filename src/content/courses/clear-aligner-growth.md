The sales page positions this course as a way to grow revenue and profit and increase clear aligner starts 15–30% in 12 months.

The exam room is where a potential patient gets information, education, and a path toward partnership. The course teaches verbal skills based on consumer psychology, and how to blend digital technology so the consumer is more motivated. Clear aligners are treated as a core part of the practice that needs attention in branding and workflow.

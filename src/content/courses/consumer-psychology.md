Today’s patients think, decide, and commit differently than they used to. Brian Wright breaks down how modern consumer behavior influences treatment decisions, and how psychology principles can be built into every stage of the patient journey. The aim is to guide patients toward confident starts without scripts that feel like pressure.

Patients see thousands of marketing messages. This course introduces 12 consumer psychology strategies that affect how patients perceive value, justify cost, and decide to move forward. It also covers how consumer behavior has changed across generations, and why a technology-driven environment needs a more intentional way of communicating.

You get a picture of the buying cycle — how understanding, perceived value, and cost work together — and how to guide patients toward clarity instead of hesitation. The second half applies those strategies across digital marketing, phone interactions, exams, financial presentations, and follow-up.

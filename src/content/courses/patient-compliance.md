Advanced six-step scripting to transform patient compliance. The sales page frames the outcome as fewer headaches from non-compliant patients, and states a goal of increasing patient compliance 30% or more.

The course is for the clinical team. It teaches leadership and culture, hospitality, sales fundamentals, consumer psychology, verbiage, and presentation. You keep access for life.

## Lessons

1. **Transforming patient compliance.** Team exercises that reframe compliance and non-compliance.
2. **The art of probing questions.** Sales technique, verbiage, hospitality, and consumer psychology, with exercises and role plays.
3. **Listening with intent and replying with empathy.** Personalizing the presentation from what you learn about the patient.
4. **Learning to take control.** Guiding the conversation.
5. **The consequence approach (CTM method).** Placing value on things patients may not see value in, applied to compliance.
6. **The solutions approach (CTM method).** The same skill set from the solutions side.
7. **The power of commitment questions.** Role plays that lock in a commitment.
8. **Putting it all together.** The lessons assembled into a script.

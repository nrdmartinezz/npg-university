In a competitive environment, meeting expectations is no longer enough. Brian Wright introduces a mindset shift that asks practices to move past “good enough” and intentionally create experiences patients remember, share, and advocate for. The course covers why going beyond expectations matters for loyalty, referrals, and growth at every stage of the patient journey.

Every patient interaction is shaped by expectations, and those expectations decide whether patients become detractors, neutral participants, or promoters of the practice. Brian Wright walks through how organizations use Net Promoter Score to measure loyalty, and why only experiences rated a 9 or 10 fuel growth through referrals.

Examples from luxury hospitality and customer service show how planning, attention to detail, and communication between departments turn ordinary moments into memorable ones. Going beyond is not accidental. Consistently exceeding expectations earns trust, loyalty, internal referrals, and room for premium pricing.

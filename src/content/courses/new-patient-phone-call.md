Patients are not only calling about treatment. They are deciding who they want to partner with. This course introduces nine consumer psychology concepts that help the team guide callers through know, like, and trust from the first conversation, so appointment commitment and conversion get stronger.

Consumer psychology influences who patients trust, who they choose, and who they schedule with. The course shows how to apply those nine strategies on the new patient phone call and position the practice as the partner of choice before the patient walks in.

When patients know, like, and trust the practice, scheduling and starting treatment get easier. The tools are meant to shape that journey on everyday phone calls.

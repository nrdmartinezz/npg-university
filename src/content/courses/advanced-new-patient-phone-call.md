Advanced phone training built to increase new patients, same-day starts, and revenue. Competition creates shoppers. This course teaches the team how to turn them into value-based buyers.

This 8-plus-hour receptionist phone training course takes the front desk through a transformation of the new patient phone call. In a competitive orthodontic and dental market, where treatment can look like a commodity, the practice wins through customer service and the ability to describe the practice in a remarkable way.

The goal is fewer no-shows and a new patient who arrives motivated and ready to say yes the same day. The team trains on sales fundamentals, customer-service language, edification and value-building, and objection handling.
